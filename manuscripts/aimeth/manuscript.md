# Testing task-conditioned development in computational-cell populations

**Research manuscript v0.1 — 3 October 2026. Complete-section working draft; core population experiments are pending. This is not a submission-ready Research Article.**

Authors, affiliations and corresponding author: to be confirmed by the project lead. Authorship, contributions, funding and competing-interest declarations require author verification before submission.

## Abstract

Large agent populations offer a route to distributed computation, but population size alone does not explain how collective organization becomes useful. We propose to test whether task-conditioned differentiation, proliferation and local signalling can construct computational organizations that improve independently assessed task performance. The framework treats a cell as a persistent computational unit and an artificial gene as an identifiable, perturbable functional module; expression records module activation. Developmental history, mesoscopic organization and functional performance are measured separately. Comparisons use independently initialized populations, explicit resource accounting and interventions on communication and developmental components. An existing interface calibration provides limited feasibility evidence: twelve DeepSeek requests returned nonempty, parseable final outputs, with mean completion latency of 2.086 seconds for eight fast-channel requests and 10.245 seconds for four deep-channel requests. These observations establish neither proof correctness nor a collective advantage. We specify the remaining experiments needed to distinguish useful development from additional sampling, routing optimization and imposed morphology. The central hypothesis—that task-conditioned organization improves capability under a controlled resource envelope—remains untested. This draft defines a falsifiable developmental research programme and reports the available engineering evidence without substituting it for scientific validation.

## Introduction

Increasing the number of computational agents expands available computation, but does not specify how that computation becomes coordinated or useful. A developmental approach asks a different question: can a population construct and revise its own organization in response to a task, and does that process improve what the population can accomplish? This question requires separating the formation of structure from the acquisition of function. A visually coherent arrangement, stable consensus or large message volume is not by itself evidence of improved problem solving.

Several research traditions make this distinction experimentally accessible. Neural cellular automata learn shared local rules that grow and repair prescribed patterns, while related models use neighboring cell states to classify a global digit. These studies establish useful mechanisms for distributed state updating, but their task inputs and training objectives remain externally specified. Physical robot swarms and models of communicating active matter further show that local interactions can organize collective morphology across spatial scales. Recent cellular-brick systems extend decentralized classification to three-dimensional hardware, while their recovery demonstrations must be distinguished from physical self-repair. Together, these findings motivate computational development without establishing that morphogenesis generally produces intelligence. [R1](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/), [R2](https://distill.pub/2020/selforg/mnist/), [R3](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/publications/morphogenesis-in-robot-swarms/), [R4](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-34484-2), [R5](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-75166-7).

Communication itself is also a learnable component rather than necessarily a natural-language dialogue. Differentiable inter-agent learning demonstrated that cooperative policies can acquire communication protocols under partial observation, while distinguishing centralized training from decentralized execution. In language-agent systems, GPTSwarm optimizes computational operations and their connections, and G-Designer learns task-conditioned communication topologies. Consequently, neither adaptive connectivity nor biological terminology alone would constitute a distinct contribution. A recent controlled comparison of agent architectures additionally reports both benefits and substantial degradations across tasks, arguing against an unconditional “more agents is better” premise. [R6](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.06676v2), [R7](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/zhuge24a.html), [R8](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/zhang25cu.html), [R9](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08296v3).

AIMeth therefore proposes an intervention-based test of developmental organization. A computational cell is defined by persistent local state, bounded computation and an explicit communication interface. Candidate artificial genes are reusable, independently perturbable functional modules; expression denotes their state-dependent activation. These operational definitions are engineering hypotheses, not claims that model weights reproduce biological genetics. Differentiation changes a cell's functional activation pattern, whereas proliferation creates a traceable descendant and incurs additional resource cost. Mesoscopic organization is measured from interaction and state dynamics rather than assigned solely from a drawing of the network.

The decisive test is whether task-conditioned development improves independently measured performance after accounting for model capacity, training, inference, communication and elapsed time. Independently initialized populations provide replication. Fixed organizations, task-adaptive graph designs and interventions that disrupt signaling or proliferation provide competing explanations and controls. Mathematics supplies one demanding evaluation domain in which claimed results can undergo proof checking; it does not define intelligence or remove the need for empirical comparisons of computational systems. The intended contribution is thus a causal account of when developmental organization helps, when it fails and what it costs. Establishing that contribution remains contingent on experiments; the present evidence does not yet support an emergence claim.

## Results and current evidence

### An operational framework separates cell development, organization and function

The present contribution is a proposed computational framework, with explicitly separate evidential levels. A cell has persistent local state and a bounded interface; a module can be activated, inhibited and independently perturbed; division creates a new identity with recorded ancestry and resource cost. These choices make the biological analogy testable rather than treating every agent, weight or message as a biological equivalent. They remain implementation proposals, not empirical evidence that developmental organization has emerged.

The proposed causal sequence is task feedback, altered module expression and local interaction, differentiation or proliferation, measurable collective organization, and a change in held-out performance. Each link requires an observation and an intervention appropriate to its claim. In particular, expression heterogeneity can reflect different prompts, geometry can be imposed by an initializer, and performance can improve through additional sampling. None of these observations alone establishes the full sequence. A developmental trajectory must therefore be interpreted alongside the interventions and resource controls in Methods.

### A small interface calibration supports feasibility, with important limits

We reanalysed the available sanitized records of the pre-existing DeepSeek cell-channel calibration. The dataset comprises eight fast-channel and four deep-channel requests to the requested and returned model identifier `deepseek-flash`. All twelve records report HTTP 200, a stop finish reason, nonempty final content, successful JSON parsing and required-key presence. The last criterion is not a complete schema or semantic validation. Response bodies were not retained; content hashes and lengths cannot reconstruct them. Accordingly, mathematical correctness, substantive task quality and full schema compliance cannot be independently reassessed from this dataset.

The fast channel had a mean completion latency of 2.086 seconds, median 2.079 seconds and observed range 1.849–2.278 seconds. The deep channel had a mean of 10.245 seconds, median 11.050 seconds and range 7.227–11.652 seconds. The fast requests used 680 prompt and 269 completion tokens; the deep requests used 492 prompt and 6,699 completion tokens, including 5,496 reasoning tokens. Combined usage was 8,140 tokens. These are provider-reported usage values, not independently measured FLOPs or verified monetary charges.

The channels differed in task prompts, reasoning mode, output ceiling and concurrency. The latency contrast is therefore descriptive, not an estimate of the causal cost of reasoning or the effect of cell intelligence. Request latencies cannot be summed or averaged into an unobserved batch throughput. Twelve requests are not twelve independently initialized populations, and no extrapolation to 10,000 active cells is justified. Supplementary Figure 1 displays all recorded observations and their accounting categories without inferential significance tests.

User-reported GLM tests support preparing a second provider block, but the corresponding raw response evidence was not independently reanalysed here. They are excluded from the quantitative comparison. Historical authentication failures, truncations and ambiguous dispatches remain separate records rather than being removed by the successful calibration. The calibration denominator is explicitly these twelve requests, not every request ever attempted in the project.

### Developmental organization and capability outcomes are pending

No verified results from the proposed developmental comparison are currently available for this manuscript. Effect estimates for developmental versus fixed organization, proliferation ablation, signal disruption and communication delay are therefore not reported. There are no measured effect sizes, confidence intervals or P values for functional emergence. A separately frozen historical 10K run has unresolved current telemetry and does not instantiate the subsequently proposed developmental protocol. It cannot be relabelled as evidence for this study.

The first population comparison will estimate the effect of the complete developmental policy under the specified envelope. A mechanistic interpretation will additionally require stronger routing comparators, matched initial conditions and targeted interventions. Reporting the results in this order will separate a possible practical benefit from an explanation of that benefit. If structure changes without improved performance, that result will contradict a functional interpretation of morphology in the tested conditions. If performance improves without the proposed developmental changes, the observed benefit will require another explanation. Neither outcome will be suppressed.

## Discussion

The scientific question is whether a system can use development to allocate and coordinate computation more effectively, not whether a network resembles a tissue. This distinction matters because biological terms can obscure ordinary engineering mechanisms. A prompt library called a genome may remain a prompt library; graph rewiring called morphogenesis may remain routing optimization. An operational developmental account becomes stronger only when its regulated modules, lineage changes and organizational consequences are measurable and when perturbing them changes independently verified function.

The available literature already supplies important counterexamples to a broad novelty claim. Learned local cellular rules can generate prescribed shapes and perform classification, physical swarms can organize morphology, and language-agent systems can optimize communication graphs. The study must therefore establish an incremental mechanism beyond renaming these constructions. The candidate contribution is a linked, task-conditioned process of differentiation and proliferation whose effects can be separated from model capacity, additional samples, communication topology and external supervision. The literature review is targeted; it is not a systematic search establishing priority.

The cell–gene mapping is an experimental design choice. This version uses persistent logical cells with perturbable modules because it can be observed through public model interfaces. It does not equate a module with a biological gene, assume that one parameter is a gene, or claim to observe model-weight adaptation. Alternative mappings—subnetworks, adapter groups, symbolic operators or agent collectives—may support different developmental mechanisms. Comparing them would require new operational definitions and resource controls. The current proposal supplies one tractable instantiation rather than a universal theory of artificial cells.

Rapid communication is also a hypothesis about collective performance, not a sufficient condition for it. Reducing message delay may permit more useful feedback before a deadline, but it can also spread an erroneous candidate faster or crowd out independent exploration. A fixed-time evaluation and a fixed-work evaluation answer different questions. Keeping them separate reveals whether an advantage arises from doing more work, performing the same work sooner, or organizing that work differently. Models with richer individual capabilities might need fewer interactions; weaker cells may require communication whose cost erases the saving. The experiment should allow either relationship.

The initial developmental-versus-fixed comparison combines differences in starting population, growth history and organization. It can establish an overall policy effect under the stated allocation, but it cannot identify a causal effect of proliferation or morphology alone. The same-resource ceiling also does not guarantee identical realized compute. A stronger analysis needs realized cost curves and prespecified interventions with matched starting states or pilot-derived schedules. Claims of synergistic collective computation require competitive alternatives, including an optimized fixed or task-adaptive routing policy and an equal-resource independent-sampling comparator. A single-agent comparator can be informative without becoming a prerequisite that one agent must solve the task first.

Mathematics is useful because a claimed theorem can be checked against explicit assumptions and proof obligations. That does not make the empirical comparison of agent organizations unnecessary. A verified lemma is a mathematical result; a reproducible increase in obtaining such results is a property of the computational system. Open-problem exploration should preserve useful negative findings and verified intermediate statements while remaining separate from a finite benchmark endpoint. Any broader claim about intelligence would also need prespecified transfer evaluations beyond mathematics.

The largest present limitation is missing central evidence. The API records show only that a small set of requests completed under particular settings. They neither validate the proposed developmental algorithm nor demonstrate a 10K capability advantage. The next manuscript revision should prioritize executable local transition rules, protected evaluation and an interpretable population comparison. If those experiments produce null or adverse effects, a useful result may still be a precise boundary on when development helps. Journal suitability will depend on the resulting evidence and significance, not the terminology or the number of manuscript revisions.

## Methods — proposed study protocol

### Computational cells and artificial genes

A computational cell is a persistent logical unit with an identifier, parent identifier, versioned local state, finite communication neighbourhood and access to a shared model backend. Cells share model weights; 10,000 cells do not imply 10,000 model copies or simultaneous inference requests. Local state comprises task constraints, evidence references, unresolved obligations, module-expression values and bounded memory. Every state update records its inputs, responsible module, resource cost and previous-state hash.

An artificial gene is an identifiable, reusable and independently perturbable functional module, rather than an arbitrary weight scalar. The initial module catalogue comprises candidate construction, constraint checking, evidence routing and division proposals. Modules expose typed inputs and outputs, measurable activation and a defined disable operation. Their implementation may combine prompts and deterministic symbolic operations. Expression is the logged activation vector over this catalogue; a proposed initial implementation uses binary gates and normalized module-selection probabilities. A cell type is a reproducible association between expression history and functional behaviour, not a role label assigned retrospectively to successful cells. Public APIs do not expose weight changes: this protocol concerns module regulation and organization, not parameter evolution or completed post-training.

### Local signalling and development

Signals contain a message identifier, sender and receiver, parent-message hash, state version, task reference, signal type, payload and expiry. Allowed types encode a candidate, constraint, evidence reference, unresolved obligation or resource request. Payloads may include symbolic expressions and dependency graphs alongside bounded text. A signal is delivered only across a recorded edge; accepting it requires schema and state-version checks. Hidden model reasoning is not a communication substrate.

At each scheduled update, a cell receives eligible neighbouring signals, updates bounded state, selects an expressed module and produces a candidate transition. A deterministic validator commits valid transitions once. Differentiation is a persistent change in module expression accompanied by changed behaviour over a preregistered observation window. Division requires a local unresolved obligation, sufficient remaining resource allowance and a validated division proposal. A daughter receives a new identity, recorded ancestry, a bounded inherited state and an explicit obligation allocation. A local rule proposes neighbour changes subject to a fixed degree limit. Exact thresholds, inheritance rules, scheduler fairness and termination conditions must be implemented and frozen; these statements do not imply that a developmental runtime already exists.

The candidate developmental policy starts with 1,024 cells and permits at most 10,000 living cells. Births, updates, memory and communication incur recorded costs. Reaching the cap is neither a success criterion nor an admission requirement. No single-cell solution is required before population execution. The proposed hundred-thousand-to-million-cell tissue scale remains a later hypothesis and is outside this experiment.

### Structure measurements and their interpretation

Each snapshot preserves the communication graph, spatial coordinates if used, ancestry, expression vectors and functional outputs. Prespecified descriptors include connected components, degree distribution, modularity, expression heterogeneity, lineage expansion and coordination of distinct modules. Layering, tubular organization or spherical aggregation require explicit geometric definitions and null comparisons. Without a coordinate-bearing model, graph motifs will not be described as demonstrated physical shapes. Coordinates, boundary conditions, imposed connections and global supervisory inputs are reported separately from organization generated by local updates.

Structure formation and functional improvement are separate outcomes. A visible pattern, increased cell count or correlated expression does not establish intelligence. Spatial transcriptomic organization may motivate initial conditions or local-rule hypotheses; a descriptive biological map cannot itself identify the causal regulatory rules of the computational system.

### Minimal population comparison

The primary comparison is between the developmental policy and a fixed population of 10,000 cells with frozen organization. Both arms use the same task inputs, backend, module catalogue, model-channel allowances and resource ceilings. Starting populations differ; the estimand is therefore the effect of the complete developmental strategy relative to the fixed policy. It does not isolate proliferation, geometry or any individual module. A null or adverse functional effect is an admissible outcome even if organized structures form.

Within each provider and task block, independently initialized population runs are assigned to policies, with execution order randomized. GLM and DeepSeek are provider blocks, not different architecture arms; comparisons are performed within providers before considering consistency across them. The independent unit is a population run on a task instance. Cells, messages, accepted requests and repeated snapshots are dependent measurements within that unit.

Mechanistic follow-up compares informative local signalling with shuffled receiver assignments under a prospectively specified, topology-preserving shuffle. Separate experiments with matched starting populations are needed to isolate developmental components. Any schedule or population trajectory borrowed for a controlled intervention must come from a frozen pilot template, not from future held-out outcomes. Such a constrained experiment estimates signalling effects under that schedule, not the unrestricted developmental-policy effect.

### Resource matching and response-speed intervention

Primary comparisons use a common resource envelope covering development, inference, communication, tools and verification. Report both allocated ceilings and realized consumption. With proprietary APIs, equal token counts do not demonstrate equal internal computation. Model identity, reasoning mode, channel allocation, requested output ceilings, billed usage and monetary cost therefore remain explicit. Unknown dispatched requests reserve their maximum authorized cost. Unused allowance is reported, not silently converted into additional attempts.

A separate speed intervention adds a fixed, preregistered delivery delay to otherwise eligible signals, without changing model identity, module code or message content. Delayed and undelayed conditions use the same update and communication limits. Report fixed-work performance and completion time separately from performance at a common wall-clock cutoff. Under asynchronous execution, delay can change event order; preserve the realized schedule and interpret this as part of the timing intervention. Host load and provider rate limits are recorded. Throughput calibration alone cannot establish that faster communication improves collective capability.

### Task separation, verification and estimation

Pilot tasks support debugging, threshold selection and variance estimation. Confirmatory tasks and reference solutions remain inaccessible to generators during this process. Before held-out evaluation, freeze task sampling, software, prompts, model identities, budgets, stopping rules, primary estimand and analysis. Reserve any optional nonmathematical transfer battery in advance. Navier–Stokes exploration is reported separately from a finite task-completion endpoint; a failed attempt on an open problem is not interchangeable with a failed benchmark theorem.

The primary endpoint is independently verified task completion under the resource envelope. On a matched task instance, estimate the difference between developmental and fixed-policy completion probabilities across independent runs. Aggregate across tasks using frozen weights. Report effect estimates, uncertainty and all allocated-run denominators; use task-block-aware estimation where tasks recur across runs. Pilot variance informs replication and precision planning. No confirmatory sample size, statistical power or detectable effect is claimed here. If replication is insufficient, report descriptive results without a confirmatory significance claim. Exploratory verified lemmas and resolved proof obligations retain their assumptions and dependency graphs and are not pooled into an improvised intelligence score.

Verification checks the exact statement, assumptions and dependencies using a trusted proof checker where applicable, or a blinded independent review procedure defined before evaluation. Numerical agreement, model consensus and schema acceptance do not constitute proof. Contradictions, missing dependencies and unverifiable claims remain unresolved. Evaluator references and hidden tests are stored outside generator mounts and retrieval. Human hints and interventions are logged.

### Failures, recovery and evidence preservation

Retain planned, allocated, dispatched, returned, protocol-accepted, verified, failed, blocked and unknown counts. The primary operational analysis treats incomplete or technically failed allocated runs as not successfully completed; report failure categories and successful-execution analyses separately. Do not replace inconvenient failures without a recorded prospective rule. Persist request preparation and dispatch before accepting responses. Resume only demonstrably undispatched requests; ambiguous dispatches remain unknown pending reconciliation, rather than being blindly retried. Archive realized prompts, final outputs, tools, event order, versions and artifact hashes in protected storage, excluding credentials and hidden reasoning. Publish sanitized aggregates and provenance. A seed alone is not a reproduction record.



### Reanalysis of existing interface records

The twelve-request calibration was not rerun for this manuscript. We selected channel, latency, return-status flags and token usage from its sanitized JSONL receipts and computed counts, arithmetic means, medians and ranges. No record in that twelve-request file was excluded. A committed derived evidence table permits reproduction of the descriptive figure; the original receipts are identified by SHA-256 `a65b43ad4795109608745e6ee8d513c584243605e6592ef49936185b86c923b4`. Different calibration conditions were not randomized as a causal comparison. We therefore report individual observations and descriptive summaries only. No model API or cluster task was dispatched during this reanalysis.
