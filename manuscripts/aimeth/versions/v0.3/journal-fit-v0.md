## AI disclosure and author preparation

AIMeth uses AI in several materially different roles: designing or running computational agents, implementing and testing code, synthesizing literature, drafting prose, and internal review. The final disclosure should distinguish those roles, identify tools/models and versions where known, locate the affected work, and describe human checking. This is a project preparation recommendation; it is not a claim that both journals prescribe that exact field list.

Calling the entire process “language editing” would misdescribe the present workflow. Likewise, internal AI-agent review must not be represented as independent human peer review. The user and qualifying human coauthors must evaluate the arguments, numerical provenance, citations and final wording, decide authorship/contributions, and approve the version intended for submission. Nature's accountability criteria cannot be satisfied merely by attaching an AI-generated assurance statement. [Authorship policy](https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/authorship).

The full Nature Portfolio AI policy page could not be retrieved because of an inaccessible authentication redirect. Consequently, this memo does not certify its current copy-editing exemption, generative-image exceptions or every AI-use condition. The initial-submission page supports the two narrower statements above. Recheck the complete current AI policy before final submission; do not infer either unrestricted permission or a blanket prohibition from an inaccessible page.

## Concrete gaps in manuscript v0.2

1. **Central scientific result:** The manuscript reports interface calibration and a corrected software contract, while developmental population outcomes remain pending. A mechanism narrative needs independently replicated functional evidence and interpretable interventions. An efficiency narrative needs actual capability–budget/time comparisons. Changing the article format cannot supply these results.
2. **Study identification:** Freeze the executable developmental policy, competitive fixed and independent-search controls, protected task/evaluator separation, resource limits, stopping rules and population/task replication plan. Describe what is imposed versus generated. Preserve failures and distinguish software fixture tests from scientific experiments.
3. **Reproducibility package:** Preserve the future study's realized inputs, final outputs, event order, software/model identities and analysis scripts. The twelve-request calibration lacks retained response bodies, so its scientific content cannot be retrospectively recreated. State that limitation; hashes do not restore missing observations. Plan reviewer access without exposing credentials or evaluation answers to generators.
4. **Publication package:** Confirm human author details, contributions, funding and conflicts; supply actual figure data and legends; prepare an honest AI-use statement, archive release and availability statements. Tailor word counts and section order only after the target article and main finding are settled.

These are AIMeth-specific assessments, not a forecast of editorial acceptance. Candidate A/B framings need a scientific core that is currently absent. Candidate C may become a methodological paper if independent validation establishes a useful contribution; it is not automatically a main-journal Research Article because its language is more conservative.
