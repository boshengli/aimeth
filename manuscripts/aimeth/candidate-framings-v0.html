Three candidate framings of the same AIMeth study

3 October 2026 · Based on manuscript v0.2, with no additional experiments or altered data. These are alternative titles, abstracts and figure narratives, not three completed papers or three scientific objectives. None is submission-ready. Abstracts deliberately describe the current evidence rather than writing anticipated results in the past tense.

A — Developmental mechanism

Draft title: Testing whether computational development improves collective problem solving

Draft abstract

How can populations of computational units develop organizations that improve collective problem solving? We formulate a testable framework in which persistent computational cells regulate reusable functional modules, exchange local signals and produce descendants with recorded ancestry. The proposed experiment separates task-conditioned development, mesoscopic organization and independently verified function. Fixed organizations, independent search and matched-initial-state interventions are needed to distinguish a developmental contribution from greater sampling, routing optimization or imposed structure. Current evidence is limited to an offline cell-state contract and twelve successful interface-calibration responses. Independent review of the contract exposed a temporal inconsistency that was corrected and covered by an expanded 24-test suite; these checks validate restricted software behaviour, not developmental intelligence. No population experiment yet establishes the hypothesized causal sequence. We define the observations and interventions required to test whether differentiation and proliferation improve held-out task performance under explicit resource constraints. Mathematics provides one verification domain, while broader claims require transfer evaluation. This framework makes developmental explanations contestable without treating biological resemblance as evidence of collective capability.

Strongest claim presently supported: Operational definitions and a tested state-contract component make parts of the proposed developmental account inspectable. The main mechanism remains a hypothesis.

Decisive missing evidence: Actual developmental trajectories plus independently replicated functional improvement; targeted interventions must distinguish proliferation, signalling and organization from extra search or routing. A whole-policy benefit alone cannot identify the full causal sequence.

Figure order: (1) hypothesis and operational definitions; (2) measured lineage, activity and graph trajectories; (3) held-out functional effects with uncertainty; (4) interpretable component/structure interventions. Figures 2–4 are pending; the calibration and contract corrections belong in supplementary evidence.

B — Functional efficiency

Draft title: Measuring capability and resource trade-offs in developmental agent organizations

Draft abstract

Agent organization may change both task capability and the resources required to obtain it. We propose an evaluation of developmental populations that treats performance, elapsed time, communication and inference expenditure as separate outcomes. Comparisons between developmental, fixed and independent-search policies would use common resource envelopes, competitive allocation rules and independently initialized population runs. Fixed-work and fixed-time interventions would distinguish useful coordination from additional computation or faster completion. Existing engineering records contain eight fast-channel and four deep-channel DeepSeek requests, with mean completion latencies of 2.086 and 10.245 seconds and combined reported usage of 8,140 tokens. Because prompts, reasoning settings, output ceilings and concurrency differ, these observations do not estimate an efficiency advantage or a causal effect of response speed. No verified population-level capability–cost curve is currently available. The proposed analysis retains failed and incomplete runs, separates allocated from realized expenditure and requires independent task verification. It would identify conditions in which development earns its overhead, including null or adverse outcomes, without equating token counts with hidden model computation.

Strongest claim presently supported: The existing twelve-request calibration is a reproducible descriptive accounting example. It identifies measurement categories, not an effective cell architecture or a provider ranking.

Decisive missing evidence: Provider-specific performance–budget curves from population runs, matched allocation and competitive search baselines, prespecified timing interventions, and enough independent tasks/runs for the claimed uncertainty. Realized-cost matching after treatment must not replace the planned contrast.

Figure order: (1) policy comparison and resource definitions; (2) capability versus allocated budget with failures included; (3) realized expenditure and completion-time decomposition; (4) fixed-work versus fixed-time intervention. Current API timings remain a supplementary feasibility figure, not Figure 2.

C — Conservative methods and evidence

Draft title: Auditable computational-cell transitions for testing developmental agent systems

Draft abstract

Research on developmental agent systems requires records that distinguish proposed mechanisms, valid state transitions and verified collective outcomes. We present an evidence-limited framework for computational cells with bounded state, typed local messages, lineage identities and explicit resource accounting. An offline, single-writer contract validates signal delivery, division and snapshot replay without invoking a model. Independent review found that the initial implementation could accept messages predating a sender's birth or a referenced state version. The corrected implementation reconstructs temporal provenance before acceptance, and its expanded 24-test suite passed locally. A separate twelve-request interface calibration supplies descriptive latency and usage records but lacks retained response bodies for substantive re-evaluation. These findings demonstrate narrow implementation checks and an auditable correction process; they establish neither durable production recovery nor developmental capability. We specify the remaining integration and evaluation requirements, including competitive population controls, protected verification and complete failure denominators. The framework provides a concrete starting point for testing developmental hypotheses while preserving a visible boundary between software feasibility and scientific evidence.

Strongest claim presently supported: A concrete defect was discovered, reproduced, corrected and regression-tested in a bounded contract. That engineering contribution is stronger than an untested emergence claim, but its novelty and wider usefulness still require demonstration.

Decisive missing evidence: An integrated policy/runtime, durable recovery validation, independent reuse or reproduction, and an evaluation showing why the method matters beyond this prototype. Without those results, this framing is a technical or methodological proposal, not a completed Nature/Science Research Article.

Figure order: (1) accepted-transition and evidence contract; (2) reproduced temporal defect and corrected boundary; (3) validation coverage, limitations and replay example; (4) research-evidence ladder showing measured versus pending claims. Population-effect panels must remain absent until measured.

如何选择

版本后续出现什么证据时优先选当前最关键缺口
A：发育机制发育过程产生稳定组织，且干预表明该过程参与了功能改善因果链及独立群体实证
B：功能效率能力—预算或耗时比较清晰，即使机制尚不能完全解释公平的群体成本与性能曲线
C：方法与证据科学效应仍弱，但可审计方法形成可复用、经独立验证的技术贡献完整实现、独立复现及方法价值

三者目前共享同一份证据。A 与 B 是同一研究的机制叙事和性能叙事，不能当作两项已完成的独立发现；C 是证据不足时更保守的文章类型选择，不能靠改标题补足科研结果。最终依据证据强度选择主叙事，保留另外两种作为修订备选；不从多版本中挑选相互矛盾的数字或结论。
