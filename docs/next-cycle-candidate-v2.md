# Next-cycle candidate · conceptual draft v2

Prepared 2026-10-02. Supersedes the scientific focus in draft v1 under D-031.
This is a conceptual revision, not a completed architecture milestone, executable profile or amendment to frozen v8.

## 1. Sole scientific goal: computational emergence

Determine whether increasing and organizing interacting computational cells produces stronger independently verified mathematical discovery and problem solving. Compare with a single-Agent system and unorganized populations under comparable total compute, and separately under a fixed wall-time budget. A single-Agent comparator is not a prerequisite for population execution. Verify valid lemmas, proof obligations and solved problems independently, preserving failures and negative results. Population runs, not constituent cells, are independent replicates. Governance is a candidate mechanism, not a separate scientific objective.

## 2. Design principle: functional cells, regulated genes and spatial organization

The proposed first operational definition of a cell is a lightweight Agent with persistent local state, a defined input/output boundary, and bounded communication. Many cells may share one model or update engine. Alternative cell granularities remain experimental options, not simultaneous primary goals.

An artificial gene is provisionally a reusable functional module or local state-update rule that can be activated, inhibited, replaced or perturbed. A parameterized operator, small subnetwork or adapter could implement such a module; an arbitrary scalar weight is not assumed to possess the required functional identity. The shared module library is the artificial genome; per-cell module activation is the expression profile. Signal reception changes expression and state; persistent differences can define functional cell types. Modules remain proposals until functional interventions establish their effects.

Use local signals represented as typed symbols, vectors or proof-state changes; natural-language serialization is optional. Layered, spherical and tubular arrangements must specify effective neighborhoods, routing, delays and feedback, rather than only visual geometry. Spatial transcriptomic patterns can inform initial expression and neighborhood priors; descriptive data alone do not identify causal regulation. Seed-based proliferation and differentiation are later candidate mechanisms; count all growth and training cost in comparisons.

## 3. Design principle: bounded local computation and fast feedback

Test whether small, specialized units exchanging compact multidimensional state can make more useful collective progress through repeated local interactions and selective long-range integration. Measure useful end-to-end mathematical progress, not output tokens per second alone. Communication delay, bandwidth, unit capability and topology are candidate explanatory factors; vary one factor at a time where possible and predefine each comparison. Faster communication or simpler units are not assumed sufficient for intelligence. Artificial delay at unchanged delivered information is one possible intervention for separating latency from information quality.

## Scientific boundaries and sources

- Differential expression, rather than routine changes in DNA sequence, is a central distinction underlying cell specialization. The artificial module/expression distinction is an engineering analogy, not a literal gene-to-weight equivalence. Background: [Developmental Biology: Differential Gene Expression](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10061/).
- Learned shared local update rules can generate multicellular patterns in [Growing Neural Cellular Automata](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/). This supports feasibility of local-rule pattern formation, not mathematical intelligence or superiority over language models.
- Human groups have exhibited measurable collective performance structure in [Woolley et al., Science, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20929725/). This does not establish intelligence at society scale, but rules out treating a claimed absence of human collective intelligence as evidence that weaker units are necessary.
