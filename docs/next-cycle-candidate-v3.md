# Next-cycle candidate · conceptual draft v3

Prepared 2026-10-02 under D-032. Supersedes v2's mathematics-centered goal and its treatment of proliferation as merely a later option. This is a conceptual revision, not an implemented architecture, completed milestone or new execution authorization.

## 1. Scientific goal: task-conditioned development and functional emergence

Simulate and test how computational cell populations, conditioned on tasks or shaped through post-training, self-organize, differentiate and proliferate into mesoscopic structures, which assemble into tissue-level organization. Determine whether this developmental process produces stronger task adaptation and problem-solving capability than isolated units or comparable unorganized populations.

Structural emergence and functional improvement are linked hypotheses requiring separate evidence. Observe the developmental trajectory, rather than only the final shape. Specify which features were imposed through initialization, global supervision or training targets, and which arose through local interactions. A tissue-shaped graph alone does not establish greater intelligence. Governance is a mechanism, not an independent objective; mathematics is one evaluation domain, not the definition of intelligence.

## 2. Design principle: regulated cells develop across scales

Retain the provisional mapping: a cell is a stateful lightweight Agent with local computation and signaling; an artificial gene is a regulatable functional module or local update rule; expression is its cell-specific activation. Shared rules, expression changes, local signals, differentiation and proliferation jointly define the developmental process. The exact implementation remains open.

Use the user's proposed working scales: thousands to tens of thousands of computational cells for mesoscopic layered, tubular or spherical structures; roughly one hundred thousand to one million cells for tissue assembled from multiple such structures. These are project scale hypotheses, not universal biological boundaries or an instruction to launch those populations now. Effective neighborhoods and information flow must accompany geometry. Spatial transcriptomic data may supply structural and expression priors; they do not alone identify developmental causality.

Proliferation must have an explicit local triggering rule, state inheritance and resource cost. Test whether task-dependent organization contributes function beyond merely adding cells, parameters or compute. Learned local rules can be globally trained; disclose that supervision rather than calling it entirely autonomous discovery. Existing related work demonstrates pattern formation from learned local rules, not general intelligence: [Growing Neural Cellular Automata](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/).

## 3. Design principle: fast interaction with task-based functional tests

Explore bounded local units exchanging compact multidimensional signals, including symbols, vectors or task states, with local recurrence and selective longer-range integration. Cell capability, response latency and topology are candidate mechanisms whose benefit must be tested, not assumed. Language output is optional.

The user's pepper pigment/capsaicin analogy motivates organization-dependent functional output; concentration alone is not an operational demonstration of general intelligence. Define capability through task achievement, adaptation and transfer. Use held-out tasks and account for post-training, growth, communication and inference cost. Compare independent population runs at comparable compute and separately under elapsed-time constraints, with organizational disruption or fixed/unorganized controls as appropriate. Keep training labels and evaluator solutions out of test-time generators.

Mathematics is attractive because claims can be settled by proof without a wet-lab experiment. It still requires exact assumptions, independently checked inference and dependencies. Numerical experiments or consensus do not prove a theorem. The separate claim that one organization improves performance remains an empirical computational comparison. Additional task domains can test whether gains generalize; none is declared implemented or frozen here.

## Execution and evidence boundary

Preserve all earlier drafts and v8's original protocol, results and stopping rules. No v8 configuration change, expanded population submission or retrospective confirmatory endpoint change follows from this revision. The candidate requires a future concrete protocol before execution. D-029 access restrictions and the existing checkpoint/receipt audit requirements remain in force. Independent mathematical evaluation, source records, failure denominators and recovery are supporting requirements.
