## Three candidate framings

- **A — Developmental mechanism:** task-conditioned local rules, differentiation/proliferation, emergent organization and causal contributions to task performance.
- **B — Functional efficiency:** how organization and response speed affect capability under matched compute and time, including costs and failure denominators.
- **C — Conservative methods and evidence:** the reproducible computational-cell framework, supported findings and limits of the emergence claim.

These are alternative narratives of one study, not three independent experiments or three additional scientific goals. Claims and numerical results must agree across variants.
